Privacy Policy: IPF Manager

Last updated: November 6, 2026

IPF Manager is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Page "It's Probably Fine". It is not offered to the public, and no one else can log in to it or connect a Page to it.

What the app does

  • It uploads and schedules videos (Reels) on the "It's Probably Fine" Page.
  • It reads the Page's own statistics, such as how many times a Reel was played, so the owner can see how each Reel performs.

What data the app uses

  • The app uses a Page access token that the Page owner granted. The token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The app reads the Page's name, its follower count and totals for its own videos, such as plays, likes and comment counts.
  • The app does not collect, store or sell personal information about people who view or interact with the Page.

Sharing

The app does not share data with anyone. It sends data only to Facebook, in order to publish the Page's videos and read its statistics.

Deleting data

The Page owner can remove the app's access at any time in Facebook settings under Business Integrations, and can delete the locally stored token. The app holds no data about anyone else. If you believe it holds information about you, contact the Page below and it will be deleted.

Contact
